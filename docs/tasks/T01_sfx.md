# Task T01 — sound effects: synthesis + mix (owner: codex)

Project: <项目根目录> (a lyric MV renderer; song = assets/audio/track.mp3, 44.1 kHz stereo, 395.41 s).
Context: docs/PLAN.md (story: one 1986 phone call), research/phone1986.md (§3 exact Japanese telephone tones).

## Input
`data/sfx.json` (generated by `node tools/sfx.mjs`; never edit it by hand): `{ events: [{ t, kind, rel, pan?, dur?, key? }] }`
- t = song seconds; rel = level in dB RELATIVE to the local loudness of the music (RMS of the mix over [t-1.5, t+1.5] s);
  pan -1..1 (default 0); dur for sustained kinds; key for dtmf.

## Write `tools/make_sfx.py` (run with `.venv/Scripts/python tools/make_sfx.py`; numpy / scipy / soundfile / librosa are installed there)
Outputs:
1. `assets/audio/sfx_stem.wav` — the effects alone (44.1 kHz stereo float or 24-bit), same length as the song.
2. `assets/audio/mix.wav` — song + effects (16-bit, 44.1 kHz stereo), peak-limited so it never clips (soft limiter on the sum; do NOT change the music's level).
3. A printed report: per event: t, kind, local music RMS dBFS, effect RMS dBFS, and the check that the effect RMS is <= music RMS + rel + 1 dB.

Synthesis per kind (deterministic: fixed numpy seed; every sound must be subtle, short attack/decay envelopes, no clicks at boundaries):
- step: footstep in fresh snow: ~90 ms granular crunch (band-passed noise 1.2-5 kHz made of 20-40 tiny grains) + a soft low thud ~140 Hz.
- door: booth folding door: short metallic creak (0.25 s, swept resonant band ~900->600 Hz) + a damped thud.
- tick: fluorescent tube starter tick: 8-15 ms click + 50 Hz/100 Hz hum burst 60 ms.
- hum: fluorescent hum bed for `dur` s: 100 Hz + harmonics, very faint, 0.4 s fades.
- breath: an exhale onto glass: 1.3 s band-passed noise (300-2500 Hz), swell then fade.
- squeak: finger on wet glass for `dur` s: 2-3 short squeaks (1.6-2.6 kHz, slightly detuned, vibrato), very soft.
- lift: plastic receiver lifted off the hook switch: two clicks 40 ms apart + tiny rattle.
- card: magnetic card drawn in by the phone: 0.3 s motor buzz (~180 Hz rich, filtered) ending in a click.
- dialtone: Japanese dial tone 400 Hz continuous for `dur` s, heard THROUGH A HANDSET (band-pass 300-3400 Hz, slight saturation).
- dtmf: DTMF pair of `key` (standard frequencies), 110 ms, handset band.
- key: the mechanical press of a phone key: 10 ms click.
- ringback: Japanese ring-back: 400 Hz amplitude-modulated by 16 Hz, ON 1.0 s (then silence), handset band.
- bell: an electronic home-phone ringer in an empty room: 1.0 s trill (two tones ~1.3 kHz/1.6 kHz alternating at 16 Hz) + small room reverb.
- relay: answering machine relay click + cassette mechanism clunk.
- tape: tape hiss + very faint motor whine for `dur` s.
- beep: answering machine beep: 1 kHz sine 0.45 s, handset band.
Unknown kinds: print a warning and skip.

Levels: effect RMS (over its own active part) = local music RMS + rel dB; additionally never louder than -24 dBFS RMS for beds (hum, tape) and -16 dBFS for the rest.

## Then
- Set `config.json -> paths.audio` to `assets/audio/mix.wav` (keep track.mp3 untouched; leave all other config alone).
- Do not modify anything under src/ or data/. Do not commit.
- Finish with a short summary of what you did and the report table.
